# County Signal

County Signal turns the existing U.S. county atlas into downloadable,
source-linked decision briefs for people who need defensible local-market
research without assembling federal datasets by hand.

## First customer

Small site-selection, public-policy, commercial-real-estate, grant-writing,
and investigative teams that need county comparisons but cannot justify a
full-time data analyst.

## Offer

- Free: explore the national map and download three county briefs per month.
- Pro — $49/month: unlimited briefs, saved county lists, and data updates.
- Team — $249/month: shared lists, branded exports, and scheduled monitoring.
- Custom — from $2,500: a sourced multi-county market study delivered in five
  business days.

## MVP

The first product feature is now the **Download brief** action in every county
detail view. It packages the active metric, county facts, detailed profile,
source link, caveats, and print styling into a portable HTML report.

## Next commercial milestones

1. Interview ten prospective buyers and collect three real report examples.
2. Add saved county lists and side-by-side comparisons.
3. Gate unlimited downloads behind Stripe billing.
4. Add branded PDF export and scheduled change alerts.
5. Sell five custom studies before expanding the self-serve feature set.

The near-term success target is five paid custom studies or $5,000 in monthly
recurring revenue. The billion-dollar goal, if it ever becomes credible, would
