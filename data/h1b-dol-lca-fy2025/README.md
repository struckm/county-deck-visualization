# DOL LCA disclosure data — FY2025

Official U.S. Department of Labor, Office of Foreign Labor Certification files retrieved on 2026-08-26 from:

https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance

Federal fiscal year 2025 covers 2024-10-01 through 2025-09-30. The four principal disclosure workbooks contain separate quarters and must be combined for a complete fiscal year. The Q4 companion worksite file contains the worksite rows used to join those quarterly cases.

## Data files

- `LCA_Disclosure_Data_FY2025_Q1.xlsx` through `LCA_Disclosure_Data_FY2025_Q4.xlsx` — quarterly application and determination records.
- `LCA_Worksites_FY2025_Q4.xlsx` — additional worksite records linked by case number.
- `LCA_Appendix_A_FY2025_Q4.xlsx` — Appendix A records for applicable employers.

## Analysis cautions

- LCA disclosure data include H-1B, H-1B1, and E-3 classifications. Filter the visa-class field to H-1B for an H-1B-only analysis.
- A certified LCA is a Department of Labor wage attestation, not proof that USCIS approved a petition or that a worker filled the position.
- Worker counts are requested positions, not necessarily unique people.
- Use the worksite records—not only the employer mailing address—for geographic analysis.
